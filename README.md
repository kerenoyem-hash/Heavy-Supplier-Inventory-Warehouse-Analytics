# Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

### CadetX Virtual Work Experience Programme

**Project Duration:** 12 Weeks  
**Project Domain:** Supply Chain, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

The Heavy Supplier, Inventory & Warehouse Analytics project is a 12-week virtual work experience project under the CadetX programme.

The project focuses on analysing supply chain, supplier, inventory, sales, purchasing and warehouse operations using data analytics, business intelligence and data science techniques.

The goal is to transform raw operational data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions, improve inventory management, evaluate supplier performance and optimise warehouse operations.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse supplier and purchasing performance.
- Evaluate inventory levels and stock movements.
- Identify slow-moving and fast-moving products.
- Analyse warehouse capacity and utilisation.
- Examine sales and customer purchasing patterns.
- Develop business KPIs for performance monitoring.
- Build analytical data models and interactive dashboards.
- Generate actionable, data-driven business recommendations.

## 5. Project Workflow

The project follows a structured 12-week analytical workflow:

1. Data Understanding and Profiling
2. Data Cleaning and Standardisation
3. Data Validation and Reconciliation
4. Data Transformation
5. Data Modelling
6. Exploratory Data Analysis
7. Business Questions and KPI Development
8. Supplier and Purchasing Analytics
9. Inventory and Warehouse Analytics
10. Sales, Customer and Financial Analytics
11. Data Science and Dashboard Development
12. Final Reporting and Business Recommendations
